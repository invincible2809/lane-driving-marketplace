Your diary, learners and lesson admin in one place.

Lane helps independent UK driving instructors manage availability, bookings, cancellations, reminders and learner progress — built for a quick look between lessons on your phone.

What it does today

Availability that respects travel time

Set weekly working hours, one-off changes, holidays and blocked time. Travel buffers and booking notice are applied automatically, including across the clock changes.

Bookings without double-booking

Create lessons yourself or let invited learners request times. Every booking is checked on the server, and the database refuses overlapping lessons even when two people try at once.

Clear cancellation terms

Learners see your cancellation terms before confirming, and the version they accepted is stored with the booking. Late cancellations are recorded, not judged.

Reminders that stay correct

Confirmation, reschedule and cancellation emails, plus reminders 48 and 24 hours before each lesson. Moving a lesson cancels the old reminders.

Progress learners can see

Record skills covered, observations and next goals after each lesson, then choose when to share a report. Your private notes are never shown to learners.

Simple payment records

Mark lessons as paid or unpaid and note how you were paid. Lane records what you enter; it does not take learner payments.

Good to know

  • Lane does not verify instructors and does not represent the DVSA. Progress stages are your own descriptive notes, not a test-readiness judgement.
  • Email is the only notification channel. SMS and WhatsApp are not available.
  • Calendar sync with Google or Microsoft is planned; Lane's own diary is the source of truth.
